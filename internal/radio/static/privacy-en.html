Privacy

Русский · Open Radio — the website openline.one/radio and the Android and iOS apps. Revision 1, October 6, 2026.

In short

There are no accounts. The air is not recorded. We don't know who you are, and we don't sell or share data with anyone.

What the service has

  • The kontakt_id cookie — a random number of your browser or app. It is used only for moderation, so that a ban works. It can't tell anyone who you are.
  • The moderation database — a hash of that cookie, bans, reports (not who reported — only a fingerprint that can't be linked to the reporter) and dates without time.
  • Short links to a frequency — the frequency, station and track name, only if someone chose to share a link.
  • Station logos — a 64×64 badge, if the host set one: link previews need it after the broadcast too. Who uploaded it is not stored; old ones are evicted, a banned station's logo is deleted.
  • Monitoring counters — how many stations and listeners, without names and without people.

What the service doesn't have

  • recordings of the air or a history of what was played;
  • letters to the host: a letter goes to the host live and is not stored anywhere; the host doesn't see who sent it;
  • IP addresses in logs, location, contacts, advertising identifiers;
  • third-party analytics or ads.

Hosting and network providers see IP addresses under their own rules.

App permissions

  • Microphone — only when you broadcast yourself and turn the mic on. The sound goes to listeners live and is not stored anywhere.
  • Notifications and background work — so the air keeps playing with the screen off; the notification shows what is playing and has a Stop button.

The host's mp3 files and PC audio

They are not uploaded to the server: they play from your device, only the sound goes on air. “PC audio” (a tab, a window or the screen) is sound only too: the browser asks for the picture, but the page turns it off right away and sends it nowhere.

Children

You must be 18 or older to broadcast. The service is not intended for children and does not collect their data.

Deleting data

Delete the cookie (clear the site or app data) and the link to your device is gone. Moderation records are tied only to the cookie hash. Questions — to the owner of the service via github.com/Dmitry-Fedotov-Dev/kontakt.
